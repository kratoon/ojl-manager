## Description

Let a seat of a shift be taken by a generic **temporary worker** (brigádník / external help): someone who is not an employee and is not tracked. It tells the engine that the seat is covered and must not be filled or overridden.

- Added **only manually** from the shift dialog, in the same employee select as a special option (for example "Brigádník").
- **Always locked**: no unlock toggle for it, the generator never replaces it and regenerate keeps it.
- Not an employee: no name, no partner, no entitlement, no fairness gap, no rule warnings, no shift counts or monthly totals.
- Fills a seat, so the other seat is still generated as usual. More than one temporary worker may be on the same shift (one per seat), unlike employees who cannot take two seats.
- Shown on the calendar tile with its own label and the lock icon, and included in the PDF export.

## Open questions

- Data model: a reserved employee id, or a separate assignment kind (for example `{ kind: 'temporary' }`) that the schema, generator and UI handle explicitly. A separate kind is cleaner, a reserved id is a smaller change.
- Should a temporary worker count towards the fairness of the employee they replaced (for example a sick day)? Default: no, the employee simply works one shift less.
- Does the pair of the displaced employee stay on the shift, or does the rotation treat the seat as simply taken?

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Shift dialog offers "temporary worker" for any seat
- [ ] A temporary worker is always locked and cannot be unlocked
- [ ] Generator and regenerate never change or fill over it, and count the seat as taken
- [ ] Not counted in fairness, month totals or rule warnings
- [ ] Month lock/unlock and shift clear handle it correctly (stays locked on unlock, removed on clear)
- [ ] Calendar tile and PDF show it
- [ ] Data schema stays backwards compatible with existing files
- [ ] Tests added
