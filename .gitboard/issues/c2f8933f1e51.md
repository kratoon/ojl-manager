## Context

Builds on ADR-a1cb97d (pairs and long/short week rotation) and ADR-aaa3ccd (fairness). The rotation per pair is FRI-SUN night, WED-THU day, MON+TUE night, FRI-SUN day, then repeats. Two pairs share the same blocks and take opposite kinds (a pair of pairs). The week 13-19 Nov 2026 was entered by hand and must not change.

## Decision

- **Rotation is the strongest preference** (pattern weight 4 against fairness 2) and the model the generator always returns to.
- **Phase comes from locked shifts**: pairs take the rotation position that best matches the kept shifts within 14 days of the start (locked on both sides, automatic ones only before the start). Pairs without evidence fall back to name order on the free positions. The anchor date no longer has to match reality.
- **Compensation may break the rotation**: an employee at least `company.pattern.compensationGap` (default 4) shifts behind the team average starts compensating and continues until the gap is closed; meanwhile off-rotation seats are fine for them.
- Compensation only applies on shifts that are **not next to the employee's own rotation shifts**. Next to them a pick only swaps places with the partner pair, makes up no shift and leaves the pairs crossed.
- Splitting a pair for a few shifts to compensate is accepted; afterwards the pairs are together again.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [x] Rotation continues from locked shifts (13-19 Nov 2026 kept)
- [x] Compensation breaks the rotation and the pairs return to it
- [x] No crossed pairs left after compensation
- [x] Decision accepted
