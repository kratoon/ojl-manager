## Context

Today each shift (date + day or night) has one employee. New requirement: **each shift has two employees**, and the schedule follows a **long/short week pattern** with rotation, for 8 employees.

## How I read the pattern (to be confirmed)

- A week has three work blocks: **MON+TUE** (2 shifts), **WED+THU** (2 shifts), **FRI+SAT+SUN** (3 shifts).
- A **short week** for an employee is WED+THU. A **long week** is MON+TUE and FRI+SAT+SUN. Weeks alternate long, short, long, short. That is 5 + 2 = 7 shifts per employee per 14 days.
- Inside a block the employee works the **same kind of shift** (all day or all night): so blocks of 3 and blocks of 2. This fits the company limit of 3 in a row and the rule that day and night are not mixed back to back.
- Two groups cover the day shifts and two the night shifts. In any week one group is on its long week and the other on its short week, so every day is covered. With 2 employees per shift and 4 groups that is **4 groups of 2 = 8 employees**.
- **Day/night rotates**: after some period a group moves from day to night and the other way round, so everyone works both kinds over time.

Example, 4 groups (A, B, C, D) of 2, day/night swapping every 2 weeks (period to be confirmed):

| Week | Day shift, long week | Day shift, short week | Night, long week | Night, short week |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | A | B | C | D |
| 2 | B | A | D | C |
| 3 | C | D | A | B |
| 4 | D | C | B | A |

(Long week = MON+TUE, FRI+SAT+SUN. Short week = WED+THU.)

## Proposal

- **Seats**: company setting `seatsPerShift`, default 2. Assignments become a list of up to that many entries per shift (`{ employeeId, source }`), the lock (manual) is per seat. An employee cannot take both seats of one shift. Existing single assignments migrate to a one-element list.
- **Rules** stay per employee. Neighbour checks (max in a row, day/night mixing) look at whether the employee works that shift, not at "the" assignee.
- **Fairness**: each shift needs two people, so the scale factor doubles (`k = seats * |W| / sum of counting shifts`); everything else in ADR-aaa3ccd is unchanged.
- **Pattern is a preference, not a hard rule** ("preferably"): a rule in the score framework (ADR-aaa3ccd) gives a high score to employees whose place in the rotation says they work this shift and a low one to the rest, with a configurable weight. Vacations, first/last shift, forbidden shifts and the other hard rules still win, and the generator fills the gaps with the best available employees, so a vacation never leaves a hole.
- The generator picks the **best two distinct candidates** per shift. Still deterministic.
- **Pattern settings** (company rules screen): anchor Monday (which week is long for which group), the week blocks, day/night rotation period, and which employees form which group.

## Open questions

1. Are the **groups fixed pairs** (always the same 2 people together, 4 groups), or should the pairing vary over time?
2. How often does **day/night rotate**: every 2 weeks (after one long and one short week), every 4 weeks, or something else?
3. How are employees **placed in groups and phase**: automatically by name order, or configured per employee (a group/position field on the employee)?
4. What happens if the number of employees is **not 8**: keep the pattern with gaps filled by fairness, or ask to configure groups?
5. Is my reading of long/short weeks and blocks right?
