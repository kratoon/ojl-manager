## Context

A "regenerate" action must fill the calendar from today to the last calendar month while following all rules, leaving today and every manual (locked) assignment untouched. Rules change over time, so the calculation must be driven by the configured rules, not hardcoded.

## Decision (proposal)

- The scheduler is a **pure, deterministic function** `generate({ employees, rules, assignments, from, to })` returning the new assignments plus a list of slots it could not fill and why. No UI or storage inside it, so it is unit-testable.
- Range: starts at the first slot **after today** (today's day and night slots are never modified) and ends at the last calendar month. Previous `auto` assignments in that range are discarded and recomputed. Manual assignments are kept and count for fairness and sequence limits.
- Strategy: walk the slots chronologically; for each empty slot take the eligible employees (all constraints pass) and choose the one with the fewest shifts so far, with a stable tie-break so results are reproducible. A slot with nobody eligible stays **empty** and is highlighted with the reason.
- Greedy can dead-end on tight rules. Accepted for the MVP; a backtracking or constraint-solver step can replace the strategy later without changing the function's interface.
- Empty (unset) slots are visually highlighted. Slots closed by company rules are shown as closed, not as unset.

## Open questions

- Is "fewest shifts so far" the right fairness goal, or should shifts be balanced per day/night kind?
- Should regeneration ask for confirmation, or show a preview before applying?

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Range and locking behaviour agreed
- [ ] Fairness strategy agreed
