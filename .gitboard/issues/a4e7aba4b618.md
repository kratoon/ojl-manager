## Context

Fairness (ADR-aaa3ccd) compares only the total number of shifts. After PTO, compensation or substitutions one employee can end up with mostly nights while the total looks even (OJL-ba0e2e7). This amends ADR-aaa3ccd.

## Decision

- **Per-kind fairness**: expected and worked shifts are tracked separately for day and for night shifts. The scale factor is computed per kind (`k_kind = seats * |W_kind| / sum of counting shifts of that kind`) and the gap per kind is `k_kind * counting_kind - worked_kind`. Counting rules (window, vacations, compensate) are the same as for the total.
- **Second rule, not a replacement**: a new rule `kind-balance` returns `clamp(5 + kindGap, 1, 10)` with its own weight, company setting `kindBalanceWeight` (default 1, range 0.5 to 10). The total fairness rule is unchanged. It never returns 0.
- **Pattern stays on top**: pattern weight 4 against fairness 2 and kind balance 1 keeps an on-pattern employee strictly ahead of any off-pattern one (worst case 43 against 34), so without compensation the rotation is reproduced exactly. Keep the kind weight below the pattern weight.
- **Cap per kind**: the fairness cap also applies to the gap of the shift's own kind (skipped while others remain), next to the cap on the total.
- **Window**: the whole calendar range, as for the total. A rolling window is not needed while the rotation keeps both kinds even.
- **Preview**: unchanged. The month totals and the preview already show day and night counts.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [x] Day and night counted separately per employee
- [x] Weight is a company setting with range validation in the scheduling settings dialog
- [x] Rotation unchanged without compensation
- [x] Decision accepted
