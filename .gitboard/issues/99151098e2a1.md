## Description

On the employee edit page add first shift, last shift and vacation periods (shift to shift, day or night granularity). Each vacation has a **Compensate** checkbox (ADR-4a9d8da), **on by default**: on = the missed shifts are made up around the PTO, off = the employee accepts fewer shifts.
