## Context

Rules keep being added and can live in different places: company-wide, per employee, or per date/shift. A shift is a specific date plus day or night (ADR-fec8e13).

## Decision

- Rules are **stored where they belong** (company config, employee record, date rules) so each can be edited in the right screen.
- At calculation time all sources are **normalised into one list of constraints**, so the engine does not care where a rule came from.
- Each rule type is a small registered unit: a zod schema plus a function that says whether an employee may work a given shift given the schedule so far. Adding a rule type means adding one unit and its tests.
- All rules are **hard constraints** for the generator. A manual assignment may break them, which shows a warning.

### Initial rule types

Company:
- Working weekdays (e.g. MON-SUN).
- Closed period: shift to shift.
- **Max shifts in a row: 3.** A "row" is the same kind of shift (day or night) on consecutive dates: 3 day shifts in a row is fine, a 4th is not.
- **No mixing of day and night back to back.** An employee who works a day shift cannot also work the night shift of that date, and cannot work a day shift right after a night shift (or a night shift right after a day shift). Neighbouring shifts in time are: day then night of the same date, night then the day of the next date.

Employee (stored on the employee record):
- First shift, last shift.
- Vacation: shift to shift (fields incl. `compensate`, see ADR-4a9d8da).
- **Override of max shifts in a row** (default: company value).
- **Override "allow one day+night pair"**: when on, the employee may work exactly one day shift and one night shift back to back, in either order (day+night or night+day). Three or more mixed shifts back to back are still not allowed (no day+night+day, no night+day+night).

Date:
- Shift fixed to an employee (this is the manual assignment).
- Shift forbidden for an employee.
