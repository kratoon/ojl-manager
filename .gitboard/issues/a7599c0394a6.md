## Context

Weights (pattern, fairness, day/night balance) were on a 0.5 to 10 scale with defaults 4, 2 and 1. There was no room below 1 for new rules such as PTO planning, and adding a weight of 1 would have made the others together as strong as the pattern (4 against 2 + 1 + 1), so the rotation could lose. Amends ADR-aaa3ccd and ADR-a4e7aba.

## Decision

- **Scale x10**: all weights are whole numbers from 1 to 100. Defaults: pattern 40, fairness 20, day/night balance 10. Only ratios matter, so behaviour is unchanged; new rules fit at 1 to 9 below the existing ones.
- **Migration**: the company settings carry `weightsVersion` (current 2). A company without it is on the old scale and its stored weights are multiplied by 10 once when loaded, then the marker is set, so a saved file is never scaled twice. Missing weights take the new defaults.
- **The rotation must win**: every rule scores 1 to 10, so the pattern always beats an off-pattern choice only while its weight is greater than the sum of all other weights. The scheduling settings dialog warns when this does not hold (it does not block saving). New rules count towards that sum.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [x] Weights use the 1 to 100 scale with new defaults
- [x] Old files migrate once and keep their behaviour
- [x] Dialog warns when the pattern weight is not above the sum of the others
- [x] Decision accepted
